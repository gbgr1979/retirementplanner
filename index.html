    How this model works & important limitations

    All amounts are illustrative. Growth is smooth, not a market simulation. Lower/higher scenarios are sensitivity checks, not confidence ranges. Tax estimates hold the 2026/27 rules constant into the future; your actual tax and future legislation can differ.

    No automatic drawdown strategy, benefit entitlement, investment recommendation, lifetime allowance protection, inheritance-tax planning, salary sacrifice, tapered annual allowance or carry-forward calculation is provided. Pension and savings tax limitations appear with the results.

    Your saved JSON file is unencrypted and may contain sensitive financial information. Keep it somewhere safe. No accounts, cookies, tracking, browser autosave or financial-data upload are used.
